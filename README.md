# The Last Normal Night

**EchoStory canon and production workspace**

A 10-track cinematic North Idaho SwampHop story beginning with an ordinary night in Coeur d’Alene and unfolding into a reality-fracture mystery centered on identity, memory, the lake, Frequency Three, and repeated versions of the protagonist.

## Canonical story question

> Which world was home in the first place — and which version of him made that decision before?

## Source-of-truth rules

- This repository is the canonical workspace for this EchoStory.
- `docs/STORY_BIBLE.md` controls story progression and reveal order.
- `docs/CONTINUITY.md` controls recurring motifs and their meanings.
- `docs/RULES.md` controls in-world fracture rules and character constraints.
- Each track directory contains its own brief, lyrics, and Suno style direction.
- New lyrics must preserve established canon unless the story bible is intentionally revised first.
- Suno lyric fields must remain under **5,000 characters including spaces, punctuation, headers, and line returns**.
- Preferred working target: **4,200–4,700 characters**.
- Story markers take priority over decorative lyrics.

## Sonic DNA

**88 BPM cinematic North Idaho SwampHop**

Deep physical sub-bass, dusty hip-hop drums, lazy bluesy guitar bends, cassette imperfection, spacious cinematic dynamics, rough intimate male vocal, selective 8-bit/digital fragments, and North Idaho environmental texture.

Avoid glossy synth-pop, pop-punk, classic-rock transformation, country accent, and parody.

## Recurring signature

The **wooden-flute growl** is both a production signature and an in-world signal: it occurs when two realities are close enough to touch.
