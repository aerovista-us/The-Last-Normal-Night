# Page 07 Location Lock — The Last Normal Night

## Purpose

Page 07 is the first human-recognition anomaly. The environment itself must remain mundane and geographically grounded so the clerk's recognition carries the unease.

## Real-world exterior reference

Use the user-supplied Coeur d'Alene commercial-strip references around **12th–13th and Lakeside**, including the **Harvest Foods / Idaho State Liquor Store** block, as the real-world location anchor.

Key visual traits to preserve:

- low one-story commercial strip
- broad surface parking lot
- utilitarian storefront massing
- ordinary neighborhood-market character
- nearby street grid and curb/sidewalk relationships
- no invented landmark-scale architecture

The location may be adapted into a modest neighborhood market / convenience-counter interior for story readability, but the exterior should no longer resemble Fleet Feet or another unrelated retail storefront.

## Story rule

Page 07 is **not** a visible supernatural event.

The sequence remains:

1. protagonist enters / buys water or gum in a normal late-night market
2. clerk looks up with genuine recognition: `You picked a good one.`
3. protagonist: `Good what?`
4. clerk returns to ordinary behavior: `Have a good night.`

The unease comes entirely from recognition.

## Prohibited drift

Do not introduce:

- Fleet Feet or shoe-store branding
- an `11:59` license plate
- hooded figures
- notes or messages
- a recurring white-sedan beat unless separately scripted
- portal light
- supernatural shadows
- broken geography
- extra story panels
- baked-in generated captions or dialogue in final art

## Production rule

Final art should be textless panel artwork placed into the locked `layouts/p07.svg` geometry. Final dialogue and captions are added during assembly.

The user-supplied real-world location images are authoritative for the exterior relationship. Generated store interiors are supporting art only and must remain plausible for the exterior context.
