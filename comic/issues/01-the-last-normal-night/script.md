# EP1 — The Last Normal Night
## Full Comic Production Script

**Series:** EchoStory: The Last Normal Night  
**Issue:** No. 1 / Track 01 — The Crossing  
**Story pages:** 24  
**Tone:** Mature psychological horror / sci-fi noir  
**Canonical trim:** 6.625 × 10.25 in  
**Full-bleed canvas:** 6.875 × 10.5 in / 2063 × 3150 px @ 300 DPI  
**Master rule:** Print-first. Final lettering added during layout. Final Rack pages derive from the same flattened masters.

## Canon lock

This issue must accomplish only the Track 1 reveal order:

- ordinary Coeur d’Alene night
- subtle anomalies that can initially be dismissed
- recurring white car
- store clerk recognizes protagonist
- transient WELCOME BACK anomaly
- clocks converge on 11:59
- first full wooden-flute reality-proximity signal
- midnight rupture
- altered but recognizable CDA
- brief unexplained Frequency Three intrusion / older-sounding voice
- doorway into old life
- protagonist consciously refuses return
- wrong-side sunrise
- phone returns to full service with WELCOME HOME

Do **not** explain Frequency Three, the white car, the doorway mechanism, the lake, older-him, or why the world recognizes him.

## Visual lock for EP1

**Protagonist:** believable North Idaho man, early-to-mid 30s, dark short hair, short stubble, practical charcoal work jacket over faded black hoodie, dark jeans, worn boots. No superhero silhouette. Tired eyes, observant rather than fearless.

**Truck:** older dark-charcoal pickup, analog-feeling dashboard, worn steering wheel, cracked plastic near radio, coffee stain in cup holder, cloth seat wear. Keep all damage/details consistent across future issues.

**White car:** older white sedan, left rear quarter-panel dent, one slightly dimmer tail light. Never spotlight it as supernatural in EP1.

**Doorway:** exact rectangular geometry, cold-white perimeter with warm old-world image inside, no ornate fantasy portal effects.

**Reality-proximity visual language:** thin irregular waveform / pressure-line motif based on the wooden-flute growl. Use only when realities approach contact.

Coeur d’Alene fractures without becoming a generic explosion. The street, lake, windows and mountains remain recognizable but misregister against themselves by inches and seconds. A hard white line tears vertically through space. Reflections show different moments. The truck appears in two almost-identical positions. Street signs double. At the moment of rupture, all sound drops out.

**NO DIALOGUE.**

Near lower edge, very small:

**SFX:** —

The absence of sound is the point.

# ISSUE 1 LETTERING / SOUND RULES

- Final dialogue, captions, phone text, clocks, signage, and radio readouts are added in layout, not trusted to generated art.
- Captions use restrained off-white boxes or borderless type; avoid ornate sci-fi UI.
- Normal-world SFX should be conventional and physical.
- Reality-proximity SFX should share a unique visual waveform treatment tied to the wooden-flute signal.
- Keep balloons sparse. Silence is an active storytelling tool.
- Profanity is allowed but should remain conversational, not performative.

# ISSUE 1 MOTIF BUDGET

Primary motifs only:
1. **11:59** — threshold preparation
2. **White car** — unexplained continuity clue
3. **Truck** — physical anchor
4. **Phone signal / WELCOME messages** — reality recognition

- Wooden-flute waveform during proximity / rupture
- Moon halo after displacement
