## Rules

- These proofs are **not** final publishable pages.
- Final art should be generated/sourced per panel rectangle, except the 14–15 spread which is authored as one composition.
- Generated panel art contains no final story lettering.
- Temporary text in these proofs exists only to prove balloon/UI/SFX space.
- `script.md` remains authoritative for dialogue, action and reveal order.
- `focus-regions.json` remains authoritative for Rack mobile reading order.
- Real CDA references and `comic/reference/CDA_LOCATION_LOCK.md` override invented location geometry.

## Final-art production order

To prove the assembly system before scaling, begin with **Page 07** because its story beat is visually mundane and easy to QA:

1. P07.1 late-night convenience-store establishing shot
2. P07.2 clerk recognizes protagonist
3. P07.3 protagonist: `Good what?`
4. P07.4 mundane two-shot; clerk almost relieved

Generate/source these as four textless plates, place them into `layouts/p07.svg`, add final lettering, QA against the storyboard and Rack focus map, then export the 2063 × 3150 flattened master.

Once Page 07 passes, proceed sequentially through Page 08 onward, then rebuild Pages 01–06 from their now-locked storyboards.
