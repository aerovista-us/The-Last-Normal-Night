# EP1 — The Last Normal Night
## Comic Art Prompt Deck

These prompts are for **art generation only**. Final dialogue, captions, clocks, phone messages, signs, radio readouts, issue title, and credits must be added in layout.

## Global style anchor

Use throughout EP1:

> Dark mature prestige comic book art, grounded cinematic realism, grimy North Idaho atmosphere, wet asphalt, lake fog, cold blue-black shadows, dirty silver highlights, selective amber sodium-vapor streetlights, rare red neon, textured ink linework with painterly digital color, subtle film grain and scratched-print texture, believable adult anatomy and faces, practical clothing, realistic old pickup interior, psychological dread rather than monster spectacle, no glossy superhero rendering, no cartoon exaggeration, no generic cyberpunk, no gore unless specifically called for. Coeur d’Alene should feel lived-in and geographically plausible. Keep the same protagonist, truck, white sedan, clothing, and doorway geometry consistent from page to page.

**Protagonist lock:** North Idaho man, early-to-mid 30s, dark short hair, short stubble, tired observant eyes, charcoal work jacket over faded black hoodie, dark jeans, worn boots.  
**Truck lock:** older dark-charcoal pickup, worn cloth seat, analog-feeling dash, cracked plastic near radio, worn steering wheel, coffee stain in cup holder.  
**White sedan lock:** older white sedan, left rear quarter-panel dent, one slightly dimmer tail light.  
**Doorway lock:** simple perfect upright rectangle, cold-white perimeter, warm old-world view within, never ornate fantasy magic.
