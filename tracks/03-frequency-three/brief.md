# Frequency Three

## Chapter
The Voice

## Canon entry state
Track 2 ends inside the truck after Frequency Three reconnects. An older version of the protagonist’s own voice proves impossible knowledge of the spare key beneath the seat and says: **Good. Then maybe you'll listen this time.**

Track 3 begins with the protagonist testing the signal instead of trusting it.

## Required story markers
- Frequency Three remains active without being selected.
- The voice sounds older but unmistakably like him.
- Older-him predicts a small event seconds before it happens.
- The protagonist tests the voice with private information only he should know.
- The answer proves genuine connection rather than a lucky guess.
- A warning arrives after the event it was meant to prevent.
- Older-him reveals he sent that warning earlier, proving transmissions arrive out of order.
- The protagonist understands communication is crossing displaced time rather than moving cleanly forward.
- Older-him gives the incomplete warning: **Do not follow the man with your shadow.**
- Final visual handoff: his reflection turns its head before he does.

## Discovery
Frequency Three carries communication across displaced time/reality. The voice is an older version of him — or something with access to memories no outsider should possess.

## Decision
He keeps listening even though he does not trust older-him.

## Consequence
Older-him can now influence his choices, but neither side can assume a warning will arrive in sequence.

## Question answered
Who is on Frequency Three? **Older-him, or a source linked intimately enough to share his private memory.**

## Question created
Why is older-him afraid of another version of him, and what does **the man with your shadow** mean?
