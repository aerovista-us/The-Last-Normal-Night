## Sonic role in the EchoStory

Frequency Three should feel like Track 1's musical memory chopped into radio packets that do not always arrive in sequence.

The underlying 88 BPM groove remains the stable reality reference while static,voice fragments and environmental sounds slip ahead of or behind it.

Older-him is not a horror voice. He must sound plausibly like the same man after years of surviving this phenomenon.

The private-memory bridge intentionally warms the production. For one moment, the impossible voice should feel emotionally familiar rather than threatening.

The wooden-flute growl occurs only at the final reflection overlap, preserving its canon meaning: two realities are close enough to touch.
