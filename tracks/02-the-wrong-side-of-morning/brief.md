# Track 02 — The Wrong Side of Morning

## Chapter
Exploration

## Canon starting point
Track 1 ends after the protagonist refuses the doorway back. Morning arrives from the wrong side. His phone has returned to service and displayed **WELCOME HOME**.

Track 1 also contains a brief unexplained Frequency Three intrusion: a voice that sounds like him says **You finally made it**. Track 2 treats that as an unexplained midnight event, not yet a confirmed relationship with older-him.

## Opening state
Alert but not panicked. He expects daylight to make the impossible understandable.

## Required markers implemented
- Sunrise appears wrong.
- Sherman / Fourth / Lakeside geometry does not match memory.
- A building exists where he knows an empty lot should be.
- Phone map disagrees with remembered CDA.
- Lake markers near Independence Point do not match his memory.
- Two strangers recognize him.
- Date/week remains the same.
- Hard alternate-history proof: the impossible brick building carries a **BUILT 1978** plaque while his own photo from last July shows the same lot empty.
- He explicitly stops asking **when** and starts asking **where**.
- Frequency Three deliberately reactivates at the end.
- The voice reveals a private fact: the spare key taped beneath his seat.

## Discovery
This is not simply future CDA. It is a different history occupying the same place and time.

## Decision
He stops trying to force a conventional timeline explanation and chooses to investigate the impossible on its own terms.

## Consequence
He becomes willing to listen when Frequency Three returns.

## Motifs
- The lake — geographic constant
- The truck — physical anchor
- Phone/WELCOME HOME — this reality has recognized him
- Frequency Three — cross-reality communication
- Wooden-flute growl appears only at the connection event

## Question answered
Is this simply the future? **No.**

## Question created
Why do people in this reality recognize him, and how does the radio voice know a fact only he should know?

## Handoff to Track 3
Older voice through Frequency Three:

**Good\nThen maybe you'll listen this time**

Track 3 begins with the protagonist testing the signal rather than trusting it.
