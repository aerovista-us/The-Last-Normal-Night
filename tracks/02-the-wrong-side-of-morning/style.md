## Sonic role in the EchoStory

Track 2 should feel like the Track 1 groove seen in daylight through slightly incorrect glass. The horror is not darkness; it is familiarity refusing to line up with memory.

The wooden-flute growl appears only when Frequency Three connects, preserving its canon meaning: two realities are close enough to touch.
