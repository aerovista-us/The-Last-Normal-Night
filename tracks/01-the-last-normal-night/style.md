# Track 1 — Suno v6 Style Direction

## Style prompt

88 BPM cinematic North Idaho SwampHop, dark hip-hop, deep physical sub-bass, dusty drums, lazy bluesy electric-guitar bends, intimate rough male vocal, natural spoken delivery, wide dynamics and dramatic silence. Begin warm, relaxed and human: nighttime lake ambience, distant conversation, cassette hiss, loose drums. Slowly corrupt the SAME groove rather than changing songs: instruments detune, bass tails stretch unnaturally, percussion slips slightly late, reversed voices appear, ambience disappears. At midnight collapse to heartbeat, glass strain, low wooden-flute growl, countdown, massive sub impact, then absolute silence and one reversed-tail inhale. Restart darker and enormous: industrial percussion, corrupted choir, warped cassette, broken 8-bit fragments, brutal low end, controlled cinematic space. Final act should feel powerful rather than horror: the protagonist chooses the strange new reality. No rock chorus, no country accent, no glossy synth-pop, no pop-punk, no parody. Keep groove heavy, patient, hypnotic and cinematic.

## Signature transition

The midnight transition is the core production test.

The wooden-flute growl begins almost subliminally beneath the countdown. At **ONE**, everything collapses into a single sub impact and true dead silence. One deep human inhale follows, but its decay reverses inward so it sounds as though something entered rather than escaped.

When the beat returns, use the same musical DNA from the opening but mutated: lower, wider, dirtier, with damaged 8-bit fragments hiding between the drums.

## Story function

The Track 1 groove becomes the musical memory of the full EchoStory. Later songs should mutate it rather than simply copy it.
