# Suno Writing Standard

## Hard limit

**Maximum 5,000 characters per lyric field, including spaces, punctuation, headers, and line returns.**

Preferred working target: **4,200–4,700 characters** to retain safety margin.

## Formatting economy

Use:

`September air`

not:

`September air.`

Avoid blank-line padding.

Use compact production headers:

`[VERSE 2|same groove detunes,drums late]`

instead of long descriptive headers unless the detail materially affects generation.

## Priority order when space gets tight

1. Required story markers
2. Hook / emotional payoff
3. Decision and consequence
4. Track-to-track handoff
5. Essential production cues
6. Decorative imagery

Story continuity wins over ornamental lines.

## Plot density

Each song should contain roughly **5–7 essential plot events**.

Do not solve multiple major mysteries in one track.

## Independence rule

Every song must still work as a song for a listener who has not heard the rest of the EchoStory.

The story should deepen the song, not replace it.

## Hook rule

Whenever possible, the chorus should gain a different emotional meaning as the story develops.

Track 1 model:

**If I knew that was the last normal night / I'd have stayed out a little longer**

- First meaning: nostalgia
- Later meaning: dread
- Final meaning: acceptance

## Pre-Suno continuity checklist

Before finalizing any track, confirm:

- Does it begin where the prior track emotionally or physically leaves us?
- Are all required chapter markers present?
- Does the protagonist make a meaningful choice?
- Does that choice have a consequence?
- Does the track answer one existing question?
- Does it create one stronger question?
- Are only 2–4 continuity motifs used?
- Are motifs behaving according to established meaning?
- Is older-him consistent with what he knows at this point?
- Does the track accidentally reveal something reserved for a later chapter?
- Does the final line/image/sound naturally hand off to the next track?
- Is the lyric field safely below 5,000 characters?
- Does the song still work musically without knowing the full story?

If all are yes, the chapter is ready for Suno.
