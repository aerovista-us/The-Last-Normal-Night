# EchoStory Rules and Character Constraints

These rules are canon unless a later chapter intentionally reveals that one is incomplete or misleading.

## Fracture Rule 1

**Never tell another version what year you came from.**

Initial reason: unknown.

Possible deeper logic: exact temporal information may help versions synchronize, locate, or overwrite one another.

## Fracture Rule 2

**Never cross the lake after midnight.**

Initial reason: unknown.

Possible deeper logic: the lake is most permeable when the threshold opens.

## Fracture Rule 3

**If your own voice calls your name from behind you, do not turn around.**

Initial reason: unknown.

Possible deeper logic: recognition/observation may complete an unstable overlap between versions.

## Meta-rule

The rules protect against something real, but they do not need to be perfectly understood by older-him.

**Older-him is experienced, not omniscient.**

That distinction is important.

# Character Rules

## The Protagonist

He is curious before he is heroic.

He notices practical details and does not immediately accept supernatural explanations. Once the evidence becomes undeniable, he adapts quickly.

Most importantly:

**He chooses.**

Every major chapter needs an active decision from him.

His defining behavior becomes refusing false binaries:
- Stay or go.
- Original or copy.
- Past or future.
- Trust older-him or reject him.
- One reality or the other.

This culminates in Track 9 when he deliberately chooses a third option.

## Older-Him

Older-him must never become a simple exposition machine.

He should:
- Know more than the protagonist.
- Know less than he pretends.
- Be genuinely trying to prevent something.
- Manipulate information because he believes the outcome justifies it.
- Occasionally be wrong because his memories belong to a different sequence of events.

His tragedy is that he has survived so long that he mistakes repetition for control.

## The Other Him / Double

Do not immediately merge the Track 4 double with older-him.

The double should remain a distinct version until proven otherwise. This preserves the possibility of multiple concurrent versions instead of reducing the story to young-him versus old-him.

# Canon vs Flexible Details

## Locked Canon

- CDA / North Idaho grounding.
- 88 BPM center.
- Track order and core reveal order.
- Midnight / 11:59 threshold.
- Frequency Three.
- Wooden-flute growl = reality proximity.
- Lake = cross-reality constant.
- Older-him exists.
- Multiple versions exist.
- The protagonist has been involved in the phenomenon before.
- WELCOME HOME means recognition.
- The first doorway was not primarily an escape.
- Older-him has been manipulating the sequence.
- The protagonist ultimately rejects simple repetition.

## Flexible Until Written

- Exact nature of the white car.
- Exact duplicate object in Track 4.
- Who first created the rules.
- Scientific vs metaphysical mechanism of the lake.
- How many versions exist.
- Whether there is truly an absolute original timeline.
- Exact cause of the protagonist’s first displacement.
- What entity/version attempted to cross in Track 9.
- Whether the wider EchoStory continues directly after Track 10.

Keep these flexible so later tracks can discover stronger ideas without breaking established canon.
